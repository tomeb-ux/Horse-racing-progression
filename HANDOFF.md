## 1. What the game is (today)

A browser-based 3D horse-racing game (Three.js r128; single self-contained HTML file or served by a small Node server) with a realistic, skill-based racing engine (fixed 120 Hz sim, ridden with the keyboard: effort, rhythm, steering, lunge, three-stage starts) and, since the expansion, a **Career Mode centred on a walkable Australian horse-training property**. Everything is procedural (no asset downloads except optional recorded commentary clips). **Quick Race** (the original arcade mode, fixed afternoon/clear look) and **Career Mode** share the same racing engine.

**Menu** (drawn over the farm as a slow showcase): Quick Race · Career Mode · Settings · How to Play.

### The career, as a player experiences it
- **Start:** a new career creates your first horse (a 5-step creator: name, look, silks, attributes from a budget, potential), $150,000 cash and a calendar starting Sat 1 Aug 2026 (season 1). You **land on the farm** and walk around it (W A S D, Shift = run, mouse look, **V** first/third person, **E** interact, **M** map, **K** skip the race-day routine, **R** reset the rig while driving, **6** training plan menu at a horse, **Esc** pause menu: Resume · Career: stable, training & records · Main menu).
- **The property** (220 × 160 m): a stable courtyard with **five loose boxes** (one per horse slot, unlocked by wins; maximum five horses), a farmhouse (walkable interior: **race-calendar board**, desk/ledger → Office, trophy cabinet, wardrobe + mirror, sofa = "Rest a day"), feed shed, **vet bay**, **wash bay**, three paddocks, a **~381 m exercise oval** with a **starting-gate practice unit**, the horse float + ute, a hardstand, the driveway and gate, gum/fig/cypress trees, fences, paths. Facility signs show upgrade levels; a map (**M**) shows the layout, you and the booked horse.
- **Individual horses:** each is a real saved horse with its own look/silks, age (ages on 1 Aug), growth curve (early / average / late), attributes **and hidden potential**, fitness, composure, readiness, training load, soreness, plan, record, and (if bought) how it was acquired. The **Inspect** card shows the real saved data.
- **Horse care (hands-on):** E at a box opens a menu — **Inspect**, **Groom**, **Feed** (stable feed free, quality feed $250), **Prepare** (tack up: saddle cloth + saddle really appear), **Lead out** (the Dutch-door leaf swings open; the horse walks out and follows on a lead rope), **Training plan** (6). Rewards are small, non-race stats (composure, recovery, readiness), once per horse per day — **care never changes racing attributes directly**. Lead a horse to a **paddock gate to turn it out** (it wanders and grazes), to the **wash bay** (hose animation, composure +3 once a day), to the oval or gate unit for training, or to the float.
- **Training (the heart of it):** (a) **hands-on** — lead the horse to the oval / gate unit, **mount**, ride one of six drills (**starting-gate, acceleration 250 m, sprint 400 m, endurance 1,200 m, settling, agility cones**) using the *real* riding controls (the real `sim.js` `Race` runs headless; the horse is drawn on the farm oval), the horse pulls up through canter/trot/walk, **dismount**, get a results screen (what moved, progress to the next point, remaining potential, load); hands-on is once a day per horse, ≤ 4 a week, nothing when tired/sore. (b) **Automatic weekly plans** (all-round, speed, stamina, starts, acceleration, sprint, agility, rest) at light/normal/hard intensity run as days pass (one session a day, a rest day a week, a taper before a booked race). Progress is **fractional per attribute**, age-curved, with diminishing returns near potential, a training-load/soreness trade-off, and a facility/staff boost capped at ×1.25. A trained horse closes ~34 % of its headroom in season 1, ~80 % by season 3, ~93 % by season 4 and never passes potential. A tired horse races up to 6 % below its ratings; a fresh, fit, unloaded one "peaks" for +2 %.
- **Race-day preparation (guided):** book a race at the **calendar board** (it shows date, venue, distance, going, **time of day and forecast**, prize, fee and eligibility — with reasons when ineligible) → objective panel steps: visit the stall → prepare → lead out → walk to the float → **load** (E at the ramp) → get in the ute → **drive** (W/S, A/D, Space handbrake; collisions with buildings and fences; a chase camera) → at the exit gate the race starts. **Shortcut:** K or "Skip the routine". Getting into the ute (or the shortcut) opens the **"Ready to go?" panel** (horse fitness, forecast, fee, warnings) → **Leave now** *saves the career and reads it back*, and only then travels (a failed save keeps the horse at home). A faint **guidance ribbon** (Settings: Full / Subtle / Off) shows the walkable route. The start is **all-or-nothing** (a failed entry restores the date, money, bills and booking).
- **Races:** the championship (Provincial → Metropolitan → Group 1 Feature series, 10 rounds a season on dated Saturdays), **66 dated open meetings per season** including a **14-race regional / grassroots circuit** at country towns (maidens, two-year-old and three-year-old races, benchmark class races; eligibility by age / wins / rating shown with reasons), the **Optional Races** screen (country vs city; entering a prize race takes you to its next dated meeting). Venues: **Royal Randwick**, **Flemington**, the original **Furlong Park**, and two cheap **country** looks with the town's name on the stand. Each event carries a stored **time of day** (early morning → mid-morning → midday → afternoon → late afternoon → **golden hour** → dusk → lit evening) and **weather** (clear / partly cloudy / overcast / light rain / rain); the race and the farm are lit and skied the same way, rain falls, turf goes dark and glossy when wet. Weather and light are **visual**; race physics are untouched (the going comes from the calendar's forecast as before). A horse runs **once a day**. After the race you **return to the farm** ("Welcome home" fade at the hardstand).
- **Finances:** purses are scaled per tier (e.g. Maiden Plate "$40,000" pays $12,000; 1st = $7,200); **entry fee** ≈ 4 % of the purse (min $400), charged at the gates; a **weekly bill** (feed & care $600/horse, property $400, $150 per facility level, staff wages) charged automatically as ONE ledger line, **sponsorship** from reputation, **milestone bonuses**, **stable-club advance** ($8,000, once a season, only when cash < $3,000) with **club debt** repaid from half of each prize; **cash can never go negative** and only changes through `CORE.apply` with a unique key (idempotent ledger). The Office has a Finances panel (itemised bill, season by category, recent prizes).
- **Staff and facilities:** seven facilities at levels 0–3 (training equipment, track surface, recovery area, stable comfort, feed & tack room, starting-gate unit, vet bay; $15k–$220k) and three hired roles (trainer, groom, vet; tiers 1–3 with wages and a speciality) with **modest, capped** effects; a stable hand is always on the property; hired staff appear as people at work (same character model, drawn only within 48 m).
- **Ageing and retirement:** horses age on 1 Aug and move along their growth curve (early/avg/late) and decline after their decline age; horses of 9+ are offered retirement, past 12 they retire automatically at the season turn (never your last or a booked horse). Retired horses go to the **Hall of Fame** with their whole record and a small tribute (15 % of value).
- **The horse market ("auctions"):** up to 6 seeded listings rotate weekly in five kinds (young prospect, developing, race-ready, late bloomer, established), with a hidden reserve price and hidden rival interest. **You see bands, not numbers** (a better trainer narrows them; a vet reveals health). **Bidding** is capped at three rounds (accept / counter / reject / seller walks). Buying pays through the ledger once (`buy:<id>`); selling pays valuation × market mood less 10 %; **custom horses** from the creator cost a fee that grows with the stable ($0 first, then $14k / $26k / $42k / $64k). Reached from the Office or by pressing E at an empty box.
- **Character:** the player is a rigged humanoid (23 bones, one `SkinnedMesh`, ~7.9k triangles, one draw call) with face, hair (6 styles), hat/helmet, three outfits (stable / training / race-day) and customisable colours, via the farmhouse **Wardrobe**.
- **Career progression:** reputation → sponsorship and prestige; wins unlock horse slots; championship points; trophies in the cabinet; persistent AI rivals and stables; head-to-head records; season rollovers (progression through the divisions by qualification); the Hall of Fame; a full dated calendar and an Office (cash, ledger, calendar, backups, export/import).

## 2. Architecture in one page

- **One page, many scripts, no bundler.** `public/index.html` holds the menus, renderer, cameras, HUD, race loop (`frame()`), Quick Race, settings and audio; the rest are plain `<script>` files with a global namespace each. Order matters (see §7). `tools/build-single.js` inlines every script into `dist/final-furlong-single.html` (**every new script needs a `<script>` tag in index.html AND a `swap()` line in the build tool**).
- **The racing engine is shared and untouched by the expansion:** `public/sim.js` (also loaded by the Node server and every test) — fixed 120 Hz steps, `Race`, tracks, horses, AI jockeys, `getTrack`. `git diff 5139a6f HEAD -- public/sim.js` is empty. Render interpolation between steps (`visPrev/visBegin/visEnd`, F7 toggle) lives in `index.html`.
- **Pure rules vs UI:** `career-core.js` (**`CORE`**, pure, Node-testable: saves, calendar, ledger, development, market, retirement, facilities, finance) and `season.js` (**`SEASON`**: races, divisions, eligibility, `LIFT` difficulty profiles) contain no DOM. `career.js` (**`CAREER`**) is the UI + glue (hub screens, market screen, Office, calendar, booking, `startBooked`, `returnHome`, `travel.*`, results bridge `onResult`, `toDef`). The farm files are the walkable world.
- **Data flows one way:** UI → `CAREER.*` wrappers → `CORE` rules → `C` (the career object) → `save()` to `localStorage.ff_career`. **Never write `C.cash` directly.**
- **Scenes** (`public/scenes.js`, **`SCENES`**): scenes `menu · stable · office · farm · race`; input contexts `race | farm | ui`. It *follows* `ui.state`: `show()` calls `SCENES.sync(state)`; hooks via `SCENES.register(name,{enter,leave})`; held keys are released on every scene change; `SCENES.enter("farm"|"career"|"menu",{instant})`; `snapshot()/mark()/check()` measure GPU geometries/textures/heap for leak tests; `SCENES.log` = last 40 transitions. `SCENE_SERVICES` (index.html) provides `leaveRace()` and `clearKeys()`. **Nothing of the race world is updated or drawn while on the farm and vice-versa** (verified by `scenes-browser`: 30 farm frames, 0 race frames). The farm has two uses of the same world: the **menu showcase** (`startShowcase`, background of the main menu, no input) and **walking** (`enter`).
- **Atmosphere** (`public/atmosphere.js`, **`ATMO`**, pure data): `ATMO.params(tod, wx)` → sun direction/colour, sky colours, haze, exposure, wetness, rain; `afternoon + clear` = the original look so Quick Race is unchanged. `GFX.setAtmosphere` (race) and `farm.js applyAtmosphere` (farm) apply it; environment maps are cached (≤ 5, LRU).
- **Headless training:** `farm-train.js` runs the real `sim.js` `Race` headless for drills and draws the horse on the farm oval.
- **Testing seams:** `FARM._test` (`step(dt,n)` simulated time, `setPos`, `stalls`, `horses`, `TRAVEL`, `atmo(tod,wx)`, `keys`), `CAREER._test` (`enterOptional` …), `FF_TEST_HOOKS=1` server flag; browser tests stub `renderer.render` for speed and drive the real game.

## 3. Save format (localStorage)

`localStorage.ff_career` is **schema v4** (v1/v2/v3 migrate idempotently, with a verified backup of the exact old text in `ff_career_v<N>_backup`; a save from a *newer* version is backed up and not overwritten; an unreadable save is kept as `ff_career_backup_<ts>`; `ff_career_pre_import_backup` before an import). Fields: every v3 field (`seed, stable, horses[], active, trophies[], wins, history[], season{n,div,seed,cur,closed,rounds[],others{}}, world[3][stables], rivals{}, h2h{}, optN, rivalSeq`) plus `day, cash, ledger[≤300], ledgerSeq, txn{key→day}, fin{season→{in,out,by}}, fac{7 facilities 0–3}, staff{trainer,groom,vet}, player{look,outfit,colours}, market{seed,listings,wk}, hof[≤60], rep, entries[], notices[], booking, farm{care{hid}, dev{hid:{prog,load,sore,plan,int,log}}, turnout{hid→paddock}}, cal{season,div,events[]}, migrated{from,at}`; each horse also has `foaled, age, growth{type,peak,from,rate}, fitness, composure, lastRaceDay, trainDay, trainedToday, plan, acquired{how,price,day}, value, history, prize, wins, places, podiums, majors, champPts`. **Cash changes only via `CORE.apply(C, key, amount, kind, note)`** (a key applies once ever; refuses negative balances; `CORE.audit` checks the ledger). Export/import is a checksummed envelope (`CORE.exportSave/parseImport`). Other keys: settings (`ff_*`), `ff_autoLevel` (AUTO quality level), `ff_sound`, `ff_comm`.

## 4. Performance work already in the game (and its budgets)

- **Racing:** fixed-step sim + render interpolation (the earlier micro-stutter fix); shaders compiled before the gates open; one render loop; the leaderboard/meters only touch the DOM when their text changes; minimap outline drawn once per race; screen-size LOD + chunked instancing for crowd/trees (`GFX.chunked`, `GFX.LODS`); GPU-animated crowd; horse/jockey LOD; **AUTO quality** controller (`GFX.AUTO`, `autoTick`: median frame time over 1.5 s windows, steps quality down/up with a 60 s back-off for failed levels, `ff_autoLevel` persisted); F8 diagnostics (avg/median/p95/p99, spikes, graph, sim-vs-render CPU split), F7 toggles smoothing.
- **Farm:** geometry merged per material per 48 m cell (`Batch`), instanced shrubs/rocks/tufts/trees with distance LOD and per-cell frustum culling, stall doors as ONE instanced mesh, the player as ONE skinned mesh (1 draw call), staff drawn only within 48 m with no shadows, paddock horses skipped beyond 70 m, the guidance ribbon = 3 draw calls only while guiding, the sun/shadow snapped to texels, `FARMNAV` A* only when the objective changes or you stray 3 m. **Budgets enforced by `tests/farm-browser.js`: ≤ 250 draw calls and ≤ 500k triangles at 8 camera positions. The overview camera is at 250 / 250 draw calls (261k triangles): there is no draw-call headroom.**
- **Atmosphere:** the sky takes its colours as uniforms; environment maps built once per look and cached; rain is one line-segment mesh animated in the vertex shader (one draw call, none when dry); wet turf = a material tint.
- **Leak discipline:** `SCENES.snapshot/mark/check`; 10 farm↔race trips grow nothing; the character rebuild disposes its geometry; ledger capped at 300; Hall of Fame at 60.
- **What has NOT been measured:** anything on real hardware (see the stutter section).

## 7. Next-generation farm (the owner's proposal — RECORDED ONLY; nothing has been started)

The owner's brief for the next major farm upgrade, as given (do not start until the stutter is understood and the owner has reviewed the game):

1. **Horses independently follow believable daily routines.**
2. **Horses exercise on the training track, graze in paddocks, recover and return to their stalls.**
3. **The player can take a horse from training to the wash bay, wash and groom it, then return it to a stall or paddock.**
4. **Stablehands perform routine activities automatically when the player is not participating.**
5. **Each horse's activities reflect its fitness, condition, age and upcoming races.**
6. **The property should feel active throughout the day without introducing unnecessary performance costs.**
7. **These activities must be enjoyable and optional rather than repetitive compulsory chores.**

Earlier farm-vision directions that still stand (recorded in `CAREER_PROGRESS.md` → "Owner directions during Phase 6–7"): a living property — horses moving naturally in stalls and paddocks, staff doing everyday activities, areas with meaningful purposes (stable, wash bay, paddocks, training facilities); hands-on training is the heart; every horse feels like an individual; the property looks more professional as the stable grows; performance first.

**Where the game is relative to this brief today (so you can scope the gap honestly):**
| Brief item | Today |
|---|---|
| Routines | **None.** Horses stand in their boxes; a horse only moves when the player leads it, or after the player *turns it out* (then it wanders/grazes in that paddock until brought in). No schedule, no time of day on the farm (one fixed look per date). |
| Exercise / graze / recover / return | Exercise is **player-ridden only** (mount → drill → dismount). Automatic weekly plans exist as *data* (`autoDay`) but nothing is *shown* — no horse is seen exercising on its own. Graze: manual turnout only. Recovery is a number (`recovery` facility, fitness/day). |
| Training → wash → groom → stall/paddock | Each step exists **separately** (lead out, mount/ride/dismount, wash bay, groom, take back to the box or a paddock gate); a horse can be led from the oval to the wash bay, but there is no guided "post-work care" flow, and grooming is a stall-side menu action (once-a-day reward). |
| Stablehands | One stable hand shuttles buckets feed shed ↔ boxes; hired groom (boxes / wash bay), trainer (oval rail) and vet (vet bay) walk between *stations* — all cosmetic, none acts on a horse or changes any state. |
| Reflects fitness / condition / age / upcoming races | The data exists per horse (`fitness, composure, readiness, load, sore, plan, age, growth, booking`); **nothing on the farm reads it** except the Inspect card and the booked-horse tack/lead logic. |
| Active all day, cheap | Distance-gated NPCs (48 m), paddock horses skipped > 70 m; **draw calls are at the 250 budget** in the overview camera. |
| Optional, not chores | Today everything is optional except the guided race-day steps (which have a skip). New activity must not become mandatory. |

**Design constraints you must respect:** (a) the 250 draw-call / 500k-triangle farm budget (no headroom — new animated horses/staff must reuse instancing or the budget must be consciously renegotiated); (b) distance gating for anything animated; (c) simulated state must be **derived/deterministic** (as `FARMDAY.stage` is derived from real state) so saves, scene switches and the booking cannot desynchronise — prefer *"where would this horse be at time t, given its data"* over a stored live simulation; (d) **autonomous activity must never change money, attributes or the career without going through the existing rules** (`CORE.groom/wash/trainSession`, the once-a-day caps) so the ledger/anti-exploit guarantees hold; (e) keep farm and race scenes separate, five-horse maximum, single-file build, no input latency/micro-stutter; (f) the booked horse must always be findable (the race-day flow depends on it); (g) activities are *offered* (E prompts, a "walk the horse" option, an optional "Follow the day" view) — not required.
