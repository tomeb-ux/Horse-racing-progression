# Final Furlong — asset & licence manifest

Everything visual in the game is **generated in code at load time** (geometry, textures, sky, crowds, trees,
grandstands, horses, jockeys). No photographs, scanned textures, downloaded 3D models, logos or broadcast graphics are
used. The racecourses are original approximations built from public written descriptions (see the reference list).

## Reference material consulted (descriptions only — nothing copied into the game)
- Royal Randwick: Wikipedia "Randwick Racecourse"; Australian Turf Club track information; architecture press on the
  2013 grandstand (Fitzpatrick + Partners; lightweight cantilevered roof with a semi-transparent fabric covering;
  aluminium, glass and timber; the Theatre of the Horse sunken parade ring; the Owners' Pavilion's floating roof).
- Flemington: Wikipedia "Flemington Racecourse"; VRC historical features; Bates Smart / Arup / Multiplex material on the
  2018 Club Stand (five levels, petal-like precast bands, roof garden); racing guides describing the pear-shaped
  2312 m course, the 450 m straight, the sweeping home turn from the 1200 m, the Straight Six and the Cup start.
